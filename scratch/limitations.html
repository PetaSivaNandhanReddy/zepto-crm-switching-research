Methodological Boundaries & Limitations

Epistemic Scope Self-Critical Academic Appraisal Contextual Grounding

1. Cross-Sectional Design

Data was collected concurrently across groups (N=184). The study cannot establish temporal ordering or prove causal switching pathways. Stated intentions serve as behavioural proxies.

2. Single-Item Measurement Scales

Promotional Pull (H4) and Inertia (H7) were measured with single indicators in questionnaire versions V1/V1.5, precluding multi-item scale reliability assessment and HTMT modeling.

3. Response-Style Elevation Bias

Current-user Likert scores exhibit general positivity bias across all items. Within-person centering successfully diagnosed this artifact, demonstrating that raw pull correlations attenuate when individual elevations are controlled.

4. Retrospective Recall in Former Users

Former-user exit reasons (n=73) rely on retrospective self-report. While geographic coverage unserviceability (Class A, n=31) is an objective physical constraint, voluntary exit reasons may suffer from rationalization.

5. Regulatory Disclosure Context

Zepto Limited's UDRHP-I filings provide audited operational KPIs and statutory risk disclosures. While legally binding under SEBI regulations, disclosures reflect management strategy rather than controlled counterfactual experiments.
