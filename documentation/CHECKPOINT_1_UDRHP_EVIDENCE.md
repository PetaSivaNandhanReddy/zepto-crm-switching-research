## 2. Media Claims Verification Deep-Dive (Controlled QC)

### Media Claim M01 (Low Switching Costs Acknowledged in UDRHP)
* **Claim:** UDRHP explicitly acknowledges low customer switching costs.
* **Final Status:** **NOT VERIFIED AS WORDED / CONTEXTUALIZED.**
* **Explanation:** UDRHP contains related risk disclosures concerning competitor promotions, alternative pricing models, merchant/delivery multi-homing, and competitive user retention risks (Risk Factors pp. 30–32). However, it does **not** explicitly establish or state the proposition that "customers have low switching costs."

### Media Claim M02 (SKU Growth)
* **Claim:** Average SKUs per dark store rose from 12,312 (FY24) to 46,623 (FY26).
* **Final Status:** **VERIFIED — PRIMARY UDRHP KPI.**
* **Exact Locations:**
  - Basis for Offer Price (Printed p. 162): Official KPI table confirms **12,312 (FY24)**, **44,341 (FY25)**, **46,623 (FY26)**.
  - MD&A (Printed p. 538): Confirms SKU breadth reached **49,602** for the 3-month period ended March 31, 2026.

### Media Claim M03 (User Cohort Retention)
* **Claim:** 45.2%–49.8% of users remain active three years after first order.
* **Final Status:** **VERIFIED — PRIMARY UDRHP COHORT EVIDENCE.**
* **Exact Context:** Verified cohort-specific retention observations in the UDRHP (Our Business, pp. 253–254), including values such as:
  - FY23 Q2 cohort: 45.2% retention at Q12 (Year 3) and 44.3% at Q15.
  - FY23 Q3 cohort: 48.1% retention at Q14.
  - FY24 Q1 cohort: 49.8% retention at Q9.
  - Descriptive multi-year cohort stabilization range: 44.3%–54.0% across quarters. (Not an independently reported customer churn rate).

### Media Claim M04 (Zepto Club vs Zepto Pass)
* **Claim:** Zepto curtailed platform-wide discounts and launched a paid membership called "Zepto Club".
* **Final Status:** **DISPROVED / CONTRADICTED.**
* **Explanation:** Definitions (p. 13) and Our Business (p. 258) confirm **"Zepto Pass"** as the defined subscription program. "Zepto Club" does not appear in the filing.

### Media Claim M10 (Cross-Category Expansion)
* **Claim:** Retained users purchase from over 18 product categories within two years.
* **Final Status:** **VERIFIED — PRIMARY UDRHP BUSINESS DISCLOSURE.**
* **Exact Location:** Our Business (Printed p. 252) verbatim text: expanding from ~6 categories in Month 1 to >12 in Year 1 and 18 categories in Year 2.

### Media Claim M11 (Delivery Partner Multi-Homing)
* **Claim:** Delivery partners operate across multiple platforms; market follows the most aggressive pricer.
* **Final Status:** **VERIFIED — COMPANY RISK DISCLOSURE.**
* **Exact Location:** Risk Factor 13 (Printed pp. 27–28, 56) directly confirms delivery partners have flexibility to join/leave and multi-home across multiple platforms.
