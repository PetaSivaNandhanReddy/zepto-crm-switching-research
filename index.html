Critical Robustness Check: Within-Person-Centering Sensitivity

Controlling for individual-level response elevation and scale contamination across perceptual switching constructs ($n = 51$).

🔍
Key Methodological Finding — Attenuation Under Mean-Centering: When perceptual scores are centered around each respondent's overall Likert mean (removing general acquiescence bias and response elevation), the strong raw correlations for ALT ($\rho = +0.685 \rightarrow -0.03$), VAR ($\rho = +0.694 \rightarrow +0.10$), PROMO ($\rho = +0.700 \rightarrow +0.23$), and SWEFFORT ($\rho = +0.721 \rightarrow +0.05$) attenuate significantly. This indicates that high raw correlations are partially driven by common-method elevation rather than pure isolated construct effects.

Final Synthesis: What the Evidence Actually Tells Us

Academic conclusions addressing the research questions with strict methodological discipline and non-causal precision.

Core Academic Findings

1. What is Statistically Associated with Switching Consideration?

Switching consideration among active quick-commerce users is primarily pull-driven. Competitor promotional intensity ($\rho = +0.700$), alternative feature appeal ($\rho = +0.685$), and competitor variety ($\rho = +0.694$) exhibit strong raw statistical co-variation with switching intention ($p < .001$), accounting for 96.5% of explained regression variance ($R^2 = 0.672$).

2. Why Response Elevation Demands Methodological Caution

When controlled for individual response elevation via within-person centering, pull correlations attenuate substantially ($\text{PROMO} \rightarrow +0.23, \text{VAR} \rightarrow +0.10, \text{ALT} \rightarrow -0.03$). This demonstrates that while promotional pull maintains genuine residual explanatory power, raw correlations overstate construct effects due to common-method response styles.

3. Why Switching Effort (H5) is Methodologically Inconclusive

The positive correlation for switching effort ($\rho = +0.721$) contradicted the theoretical hypothesis. Centering reduced this association to $\rho = +0.05$. This confirms that single-item switching effort captured generalized cognitive strain rather than protective retention barriers. Management must not interpret high effort as a retention asset.

4. Involuntary Relocation vs. Voluntary Competitive Churn

Former users cannot be treated as a monolithic churn group. $42.5\%$ ($n=31$) exited involuntarily due to geographic relocation outside Zepto's delivery network. Among voluntary switchers ($n=32$), $50.0\%$ cited catalog deficiencies (out-of-stock items or limited assortment), directly mirroring Zepto's massive capital expenditure to scale dark-store SKUs from 12K to 46K+.

Research Limitations & Methodological Boundaries

Explicit boundaries of the empirical findings and recommendations for future academic inquiry.

Primary Methodological Limitations

  • Current User Sub-Sample Size: Active current-user hypothesis testing is based on $n = 51$ respondents. While statistically adequate for non-parametric Spearman tests and exploratory regression, larger multi-city panels are recommended for structural equation modeling (SEM).
  • Cross-Sectional Self-Report: Survey data captures self-reported perceptual states at a single point in time. It measures switching intention and recall of past exits, not passively logged transaction telemetry.
  • Measurement Overlap & Scale Contamination: Single-item operationalization of complex mooring constructs (e.g., SWEFFORT) resulted in scale artifacts, highlighting the need for multi-item validated psychometric instruments.

Secondary & Regulatory Evidence Boundaries

  • Company Disclosures as Corporate Representation: UDRHP data (E01–E25) represents certified management representations for public offering purposes. They illustrate platform-level capacity and growth rather than customer-level micro-causality.
  • Non-Adopters are Non-Switchers: Never-users ($n = 60$) provide valuable context on general quick-commerce adoption barriers, but must never be analyzed as brand switchers.
  • Third-Party Industry Reports: Redseer statements cited within the UDRHP reflect company-commissioned industry research and should not be cited as independent academic verification.
